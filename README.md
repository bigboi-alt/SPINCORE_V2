SPINCORE: To the Edge of the Void
Welcome to SPINCORE, the ultimate cosmic incremental experience. Start from a humble planet and scale your way through the multiverse until the laws of physics themselves become your plaything.

In SPINCORE, every click brings you closer to cosmic dominance, and every rebirth is just a new beginning.

🚀 **Core Gameplay**
Click & Automate: Start by manually spinning to generate resources. Purchase upgrades to automate production and watch your numbers skyrocket while you’re AFK.

Prestige & Rebirth: When progress slows, trigger a Rebirth. You’ll earn Prestige Points to spend in the Shop on permanent, game-changing buffs.

The Dimension Climb: Conquer your home system to reach The Void. Unlocking it opens the gateway to 10 unique Dimensions, each housing 8 distinct planets.

Planet Buffs: Every planet you discover provides its own unique boost to your economy or production speed.
--------------------
🛠️ **Material Inventory & Crafting**
As you idle on various planets, you will harvest specialized materials. These are essential for crafting powerful Spinners—the heart of your industrial machine.

Available Materials:
Common: Iron (Common metal), Copper (Conductive material), Silicon (Tech component).

Advanced: Titanium (Strong lightweight metal), Helium-3 (Fusion fuel), Regolith (Lunar soil).

Elemental: Iron Oxide, Carbon, Water Ice, Hydrogen, Ammonia, Methane.

Rare Minerals: Ice Crystals, Silicates, Exotic Matter (Unknown properties).

Deep Space: Diamond Rain (Compressed carbon), Frozen Methane, Quantum Ice.

End-Game Essences: Void Essence (Pure nothingness), Dark Matter (Invisible mass), Antimatter (Opposite of matter).
-------------------------
✨** Transcendence (End Game)**
Once you have conquered all 10 dimensions, you face the final choice. Transcendence is permanent. You must choose one of three eternal paths—each granting massive buffs and a secret, "cracked" Spinner exclusive to that path.

Note: You only receive the Spinner for the path you choose. Choose wisely!
--------------------------------

Scaling mastery for completionists.

All multipliers scale with total planets.
